Shot Analysis

Shooting percentages change throughout the season. Use ESPN's live NBA stats page for verified field-goal, three-point, and free-throw percentages rather than example numbers.

How to read the numbers

Shooting percentage is shots made divided by shots attempted, multiplied by 100. For example, 25 makes out of 50 attempts is 50%. ESPN lists player and team shooting statistics.
